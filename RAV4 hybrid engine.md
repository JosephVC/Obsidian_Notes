- parallel hybrid system that uses both the gas and electric motor
	- combined 219hp
	- contrast with a series hybrid, which runs off the battery alone and just uses the gas engine to charge the batteries and power electric motors


- Gas engine
	- 2.5L four cylinder
	- can turn on to charge the battery


- Nickel-Metal hydride battery
	- 2020 or newer models have batteries capable of lasting 150,000 or 10 years (whatever is first)
	- can go up to 42 miles on battery alone
	- traction battery
	- recharges while driving
	- charges via the gas engine and also through regenerative braking that allows the electric motor to convert the braking motion into electricity
