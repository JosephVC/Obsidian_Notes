there are layers to a PDF, and as you make more edits and do more things to a PDF - add fields, text boxes, etc. - there are more things that can be changed and also go wrong with the PDF

When you flatten a PDF, all the other layers are compressed into one layer. while this takes away the capacity to edit the current objects - there are no fields or new boxes to edit - the flattened PDF will be easier to process and overall work with as all the extra layers that could have been a problem to open or with with in certain editors are now gone. uploading the document will be easier for the same reason

an easy way to do this would be to print to the system's stock PDF printer, such as Microsoft Print to PDF

scanned documents are already flat

you cannot unflatten a PDF - the fomerlly editable fields are now gone
